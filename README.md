![The world in one day — Friday, 20 November 2026](entry.png)

## The six stories

1. **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — The International Court of Justice ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the first such finding against a state by the court.
2. **Trump, Xi & Putin summit** — The three leaders held their first three-way meeting in Shenzhen and issued a four-point joint peace declaration.
3. **Markets tumble** — Japan's Nikkei fell 4.8% and the S&P 500 dropped for a fifth straight day as investors doubted AI valuations.
4. **Missile hits Riyadh tower** — Houthi missiles struck Riyadh's financial district, damaging the Public Investment Fund tower.
5. **GTA 6 tops $2.3 billion** — Launch sales passed $2.3 billion, a record.
6. **Kenya beats Ebola** — Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient, 37 days after its first case.

## How the entry works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory. It writes the same bytes on every run.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
